01 / About

The universe is bigger than a textbook.

Novae Astro Club brings astronomy closer to people through observation, exploration, learning and community.

We are the astronomy community by Novae Glamp — a dark-sky campsite in Bhongir, 60 km from Hyderabad, where city lights fade and planets, star clusters and meteor showers come alive through a telescope.

We welcome school students, college students, families, astrophotographers, science lovers — and anyone curious about the universe.

  • 60km from Hyderabad, under darker skies
  • 0entrance tests. No prior knowledge needed
  • 24live sessions a year
  • 12e-magazines a year

Vision

We envision a community where anyone can discover astronomy through direct experience — looking through a telescope, identifying a constellation, photographing the night sky, or simply standing beneath a sky full of stars.

Learning themes

Seven stars. Seven questions.

The Saptarishi — the Big Dipper — has seven bright stars. We’ve given each one a question that keeps us looking up.

  1. Alkaid · MarichiHow are stars born?
  2. Mizar · VasishthaWhat happens inside a black hole?
  3. Alioth · AngirasHow do planets form?
  4. Megrez · AtriWhy do galaxies collide?
  5. Dubhe · KratuHow far can we see into the universe?
  6. Merak · PulahaAre we alone?
  7. Phecda · PulastyaHow do astronomers discover new worlds?
